#On the phone
1Start recording, open House Scan, go through onboarding. Stand about 6 ft in front of the meter and tap it (F-1).
2Step in until the meter fills the circle and hold still for the close-up (C-1).
3Back out to 6–8 ft. It says "walk left" first: go to the left corner, then all the way right. Tilt down when told so the turf is in view.
4Along the way: Mark something for GAS, the window and the "AC unit". At each corner, answer the end question with "turns a corner" (W-7, W-8). Then the list, upload, result and AR view.

The wall is probably under 20 ft, so "Wall ends here" may never appear (known bug B-06). If it doesn't, say so on the recording and use "Can't get there" at the corner.
